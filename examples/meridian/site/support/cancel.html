Cancel your service

You can cancel any time in the app or by calling 611. Devices on a payment plan become due in full.

How to cancel

  1. Open the app and choose Account.
  2. Choose Cancel service.
  3. Confirm. Your service ends at the end of the bill cycle.

Before you go, check whether you qualify for loyalty pricing.
